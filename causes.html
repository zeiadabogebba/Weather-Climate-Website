The basic science

Why the planet warms

The greenhouse effect is natural and necessary for life, but extra carbon dioxide, methane, and nitrous oxide make it stronger. More heat stays near Earth's surface, raising average temperatures and increasing extremes.

Most modern warming comes from burning coal, oil, and gas for electricity, transport, industry, heating, and manufacturing.

Main drivers

Where emissions come from

Energy production

Power plants that burn fossil fuels release large amounts of carbon dioxide while producing electricity and heat.

Transportation

Cars, trucks, ships, and aircraft often rely on oil-based fuels that add greenhouse gases to the atmosphere.

Deforestation

Removing forests releases stored carbon and reduces Earth's ability to absorb future emissions.

Food systems

Agriculture can release methane and nitrous oxide through livestock, rice farming, fertilizers, and waste.

Knowing the causes makes climate action more focused and fair.
