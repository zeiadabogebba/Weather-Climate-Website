Impacts

Climate change makes familiar risks stronger and less predictable.

Heat, water, oceans, crops, health, and infrastructure are all affected by a warming world.

Risk patternMore extremes
Ocean signalRising seas
Human impactHealth stress
01

Extreme heat

Hotter days and longer heat waves can harm health, reduce outdoor productivity, strain electricity systems, and worsen drought.

02

Water disruption

Warmer air holds more moisture, which can intensify heavy rainfall. At the same time, higher temperatures increase evaporation and can deepen drought.

03

Ocean change

Oceans absorb extra heat and carbon dioxide, contributing to sea-level rise, coral bleaching, and acidification that affects marine life.

04

Food and health

Changing rainfall, heat stress, and pests can reduce crop reliability, while smoke, heat, and disease spread can affect public health.

Climate impacts are not equal everywhere.

Communities with fewer resources often face the greatest risks, even when they contributed least to the problem. Fair climate planning protects the most vulnerable first.

Prepared communities can reduce harm and recover faster.
