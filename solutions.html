Solutions

Climate action is a mix of reducing emissions and preparing wisely.

Good solutions improve daily life while lowering long-term risk.

Clean energy

Solar, wind, storage, efficient grids, and clean heating reduce dependence on fossil fuels.

Efficient buildings

Insulation, smart cooling, LED lighting, and efficient appliances cut energy use and bills.

Better transport

Walking, cycling, transit, electric vehicles, and compact cities reduce pollution and congestion.

Nature restoration

Healthy forests, wetlands, farms, and urban trees store carbon and protect communities.

Adaptation plans

Heat alerts, flood maps, resilient buildings, and emergency planning reduce climate damage.

Public action

Voting, local projects, school programs, and community science create pressure for lasting change.

Try this week

Small steps that build momentum

  • Track electricity use and turn off wasted power.
  • Choose one low-carbon trip by walking, biking, sharing, or using transit.
  • Reduce food waste by planning meals before shopping.
  • Plant shade-friendly greenery where it can survive and help cool the area.

The most effective action is sustained, shared, and practical.
